Keys are validated with POST /api/providers and stored in your OS keychain — never in the repo. You can name a credential (alias) and set its plan once it's added.
Why providers first

Dispatch is local-first: trivial and standard tasks go to Ollama, only complex ones reach a cloud model. With Ollama alone the factory already works — for free.
