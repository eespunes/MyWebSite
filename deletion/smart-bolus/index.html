ACCOUNT & DATA

Request deletion from Smart Bolus

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the app. Use the email associated with your account so the developer can locate it and verify ownership before making changes.

Deletion request details

For selected data, describe the category to delete. Do not include passwords, health measurements or financial records.

Your request is sent through FormSubmit to our Gmail inbox. You may be asked to complete a spam-prevention check. Submit a request for the developer to review. Submitting this form does not automatically delete your account or data.

You can also email erik.espunyes7@gmail.com directly with “Smart Bolus deletion request” and your account email.

What happens next

The developer will need to verify account ownership and clarify the scope of your request. Requests are actioned within 30 days of identity verification. We confirm completion by email.

Deletion scope

The current in-app deletion flow removes your user profile, notification tokens and account memberships, and deletes patient groups you own with their associated records. Leaving a patient group you do not own does not delete that patient’s account.

What is retained

Limited security and backup logs may remain for up to 90 days for fraud prevention, security and legal compliance, then are deleted. Records belonging to other users are not deleted by your request. Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove data stored on your device. Copies you exported or that recipients saved separately must be removed separately.
