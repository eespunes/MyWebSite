This Privacy Policy explains how the Thrive mobile app (package cat.eespunes.thrive), operated by Erik Espuñes Jubero (“we”, “us”), collects, uses, stores and shares your data. Thrive is a family budgeting app that lets members of a household share a single budget workspace across devices.

01

Data we collect

CategoryExamples
Account identity Your email address (email/password sign-in) or your Google account profile — name, email address and profile photo — if you choose “Continue with Google”.
Authentication credentials Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never stored by us in readable form. The optional family join password is stored only as a salted hash on our backend and is never readable by apps or other users.
Profile data Display name, initials, sign-in provider, and an optional avatar photo you select from your device.
Budget / workspace content The financial information you enter: family name and members, income, expenses, savings, categories and related amounts.
App state Lightweight preferences used to restore your view — active family, selected year/month and last screen.
On-device storage Per-device preferences (via SharedPreferences), and for signed-in families no copy of your family data — it is always loaded from and saved to the cloud. In local/demo mode your data is kept here instead. This stays on your device.

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or device sensor data, and Thrive contains no advertising SDKs.

02

Why we collect it (purpose & legal basis)

  • Provide the service — authenticate you and sync your family budget across your devices (performance of a contract).
  • Family collaboration — let invited members share one budget workspace.
  • Security — verify family join requests server-side so only authorised people join a household (legitimate interest).
03

Where and how data is stored

Thrive uses Google Firebase as its backend:

  • Firebase Authentication — manages your identity and credentials.
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your profile (users/{uid}) and shared family workspaces (families/{familyId}).
  • Cloud Functions — verify family join passwords; the salted credential mapping is private to the backend.

This data is hosted by Google Cloud (primary processing region europe-west1). Google processes data on our behalf under Google’s security and privacy terms.

04

Sharing with third parties

  • Google (Firebase / Google Cloud) — our infrastructure processor.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose it; governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Within your family — budget content is visible to the members of the family workspace you join or create.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

05

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time — see our Account Deletion page for the full process, scope and timelines. In summary, deleting your account removes your profile, deletes any family you are the sole member of, and removes your Firebase Authentication record. Limited security/backup logs may persist for up to 90 days for legal and security reasons.
