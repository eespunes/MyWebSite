Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

01

Who is responsible

Smart Bolus is developed by Erik Espuñes Jubero. For privacy questions or requests, contact erik.espunyes7@gmail.com.

02

Data the app handles

Account identity and sign-in details; patient accounts and caregiver memberships; glucose and carbohydrate inputs, insulin dose logs, bolus settings and reminders; notification registration tokens. Health-related entries are sensitive personal data.

03

How data is used

Provide the app’s calculations, history, reminders, device synchronisation and patient/caregiver sharing.

04

Services and sharing

The current app uses Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check and Cloud Messaging, with optional Google sign-in. Patient information is accessible to caregivers granted access to the patient account.

05

Storage and retention

We keep account data and synchronised content while your account is active. Deletion requests are actioned within 30 days of identity verification. Limited security and backup logs may remain for up to 90 days for fraud prevention, security and legal compliance, then are deleted. These limits also apply to health and fitness data; we do not impose an additional medical-record retention period simply because the app handles health information.

06

Security and storage

Cloud account information is stored using Google Firebase. Authentication and access controls restrict access to account data; passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication rather than stored by us in readable form. App preferences and cached records may also be stored on your device. Protect your device and account credentials. No electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

07

Sale and advertising

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We use information to provide the app, maintain security and handle support and privacy requests.

08

Sensitive health information

Smart Bolus handles glucose readings, carbohydrate and meal entries, insulin doses and dose history, insulin-to-carbohydrate ratios, sensitivity and target settings, reminders and patient/caregiver relationships. These details are used for the calculations, records, synchronisation and sharing features you use. They are not used for advertising or marketing profiles.

Caregivers with access to a patient account can view information available to their role. Only grant access to people you trust. You can remove caregiver access through the app’s sharing controls. Removing access cannot erase copies a recipient has already saved or exported.

You can disable notifications in device settings; reminders will then be affected. Notification previews may be visible on your device’s lock screen, depending on your settings. Exported reports are controlled by you and the recipients you choose.

09

Health-data choices and consent

You may request access, correction, export or deletion of health records. Where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw that consent by contacting us or requesting deletion; withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out. Features that need the deleted information may no longer work. This privacy policy does not itself obtain consent or replace any consent required within the app.

10

Patient information entered by caregivers

Only enter or share another person’s health information when you have appropriate authority to do so. A parent, guardian or authorised representative can contact us to exercise the patient’s privacy rights; we verify their authority before disclosing or deleting records.

11

Your choices

Contact the developer to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to ask about sharing and consent. Where applicable, you may also request a portable copy, restriction of processing, or object to processing, and lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Do not include passwords, medical records or financial details in your request.

12

Deletion and shared data

The current in-app deletion flow removes your user profile, notification tokens and account memberships, and deletes patient groups you own with their associated records. Leaving a patient group you do not own does not delete that patient’s account.

Removing the app from your device does not by itself delete a cloud account. Copies you exported or shared outside the app may need to be removed separately.

13

Requests through this page

The deletion form sends your app name, account email, requested deletion scope and optional details through FormSubmit to our Gmail inbox. We use these details to verify and handle your request. Do not submit health records, financial records or passwords.

14

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
