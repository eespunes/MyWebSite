About Me

Since I was very little, I played videogames. I do not have any memory in which I was not holding a controller or a game device.
My real interest in making videogames, started the summer I finished secondary school (ESO in Spain) and I was about to start the baccalaureate, around 2014. I remember playing GTA V, and thinking, how they did it. I also would not be interested in videogames if was not for Assassin’s Creed, my favourite game saga. Since then, I started learning how to code videogames and decided to study computer science. However, I discovered a double university degree in computer science and videogames.
I do consider myself a good and hardworking programmer, always motivated to learn more programming and find the best solutions for the challenges I find along the way.
